Coffee Plus Much More

Nespresso introduces Essenza Plus, perfect for an espresso, a Lungo, an Americano, or even tea with the hot water option. And to show you how much it cares about your coffee, it even sends out an alert when it needs descaling. A little plus, which might just be essential.

Introducing the Nespresso Essenza Plus

4 programmable coffee selections

4 cup sizes plus hot water - Espresso, Lungo, Americano & Americano XL

Adjustable water tank

Move the water tank around the machine to fit any kitchen

Descaling alert

Receive an alert when your machine needs descaling

Connectivity

Never run out of your favorite capsules thanks to a re-order button embedded into the machine. The connectivity function also you to receive maintenance notifications and customize recipes on the machine
